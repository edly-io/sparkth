# Getting started

Sparkth's Compose feature helps you turn an idea into a course. You describe the course to an
AI assistant, optionally attach documents for it to draw on, and it builds the course for you
in your LMS.

This guide is for instructors and administrators using Sparkth in the browser. If you run the
server yourself, see the [developer docs](../guides/configuration.md) instead.

## Find your way around

After you sign in, the sidebar on the left takes you everywhere:

| Sidebar entry | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Create Course** | The AI assistant that writes your course. Shown once Compose is turned on in **My Plugins**. |
| **Resources** | Files you have imported from an online drive for the assistant to use. |
| **My Plugins** | Turn features on and configure them. The **Settings** link in your user menu opens the same page. |
| **AI Keys** | The keys Sparkth uses to call your AI provider. |
| **Analytics** | Usage figures. Shown only if your account has analytics access. |
| **Admin** | Administrator features, such as managing the email whitelist. Shown only to administrators. |

The **Dashboard** lists the plugins you have turned on that have a page of their own. LMS
plugins never appear there: you configure them in **My Plugins** and use them through the
assistant.

## From first sign-in to a course

1. [Sign in](account.md) to Sparkth.
2. [Add an AI key](ai-keys.md) for your AI provider.
3. In [My Plugins](plugins.md), turn on Compose and select that key in its configuration.
4. Turn on and configure the plugin for your LMS.
5. Open **Create Course** and describe the course you want.

## Supported integrations

As of the latest release, Sparkth connects to:

| Kind | Supported |
|---|---|
| LMS | Canvas, Open edX, Moodle |
| Online drive | Google Drive |
| Team chat | Slack |
