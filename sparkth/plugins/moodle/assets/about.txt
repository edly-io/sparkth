Moodle vocabulary, structure and behaviour.

Structure: Course -> Sections -> Activities.
- Course: identified by an integer id; has a fullname, a shortname that must be unique on
  the site, and a category (categoryid). Courses use the "topics" format.
- Section: a numbered block of the course page (a topic/week). Sections are appended in
  order; place activities in one by its sectionnum.
- Activity: an item inside a section. These tools create Page activities (an HTML lesson)
  and Quiz activities. Quiz questions are multichoice or truefalse and are stored in the
  course's question bank.

Publish: Moodle has no draft/publish step. Everything these tools create is visible
immediately to users enrolled in the course; learners who are not enrolled see nothing.
Visibility is controlled with show/hide in Moodle itself, which these tools do not change.
When the author asks to "publish a course to Moodle", they mean create it there.
