Building interactive PXC learning activities that learners use inside a course,
such as quizzes, matching or sorting exercises, flashcards, polls, fill-in-the-blank
exercises or small simulations. This covers describing an activity, answering the
assistant's questions about it, asking to build, change, fix or preview an activity,
and listing or reopening activities the author built. Short replies, choices or
confirmations that answer the assistant's own questions about an activity belong
here too. Placing or adding a built activity into a course or an LMS unit is not
part of this job: that belongs to the conversation that designs the course.
